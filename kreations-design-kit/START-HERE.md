# Start here

**Kreations Design Kit** is an art director for AI builds. Ask your assistant for a website, landing page, portfolio, HTML tool or calculator, a designed document or a slide deck, and the kit shapes the look, tone, copy, motion and imagery. Other skills (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, frameworks) still handle the file itself.

## What to expect
- **It asks first when your brand is missing.** Expect a few short questions (logo, colours, feel, what must be included) and 2–3 style directions to pick from. Say "just build it" to skip them.
- **Your brand wins.** Give it your logo, colours, fonts, current site or photos, and it builds from those. Rebuilding a site keeps its pages and real content.
- **It never makes up facts.** Prices, hours, results and reviews come only from you. Anything missing shows as a marked placeholder, with a slim draft bar at the top.
- **It gives you colour options.** Without brand colours, it picks from a shortlist for your kind of project and names two alternatives you can ask for instead.
- **It stays honest about checks.** If it couldn't look at the result, it says so.

## Get good results
1. **Say the job and the feel in one line:** "A one-page site for my friend's bakery, warm and playful, one HTML file."
2. **Pick your motion words:** "showy" gets a full journey, saying nothing gets one standout moment, and "clean" or "subtle" keeps it gentle.
3. **For decks, say who it's for.** "For work" gets fonts every Windows and Mac already has. Otherwise it asks once about custom fonts.
4. **For a final version, give the real details** (hours, prices, contact) so the placeholders go away.
5. **Use a strong model and setting for full sites.** Small or fast settings work, but they check less.

## Make it yours (optional)
Copy `personal.example.md` to `personal.md` in this folder and fill it in: your name, your usual host, your own rules. The kit reads it first. It wins over the defaults, but not over what you ask for or a client's existing brand. In apps that load only one file (Open WebUI, ChatGPT Projects, system prompts), paste your `personal.md` under SKILL.md.
