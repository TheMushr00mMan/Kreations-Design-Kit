# Font library (curated picks, pick freely)

About 95 curated fonts, grouped by mood. **Pick freely:** these are favourites, not a required list, and any good font outside it is fine.

## Rules
- **One display face per page, for headlines only.** Pair it with a clean text face for body copy (see "Everyday sans"). A third face (mono or small caps) only for labels, if the tone wants it.
- **Build with free fonts only** (websites, tools, PDFs). Those builds use a free, embeddable font (Fontsource / Google Fonts, all verified on npm). Editable Office files are the exception: see the no-install rule at the end of this list. Most of the curated picks are **paid**; don't assume the user owns them.
- **Paid picks go in your reply, never on the site.** When one of the paid picks would be the ideal choice, build with the free near-match and add one line to your finishing note, e.g. *"Upgrade: Melfira (paid) would make this headline even more refined."* Never mention paid fonts, licences or upgrades inside the page itself.
- **Professional and client work uses crisp faces** (marked ✦ below): sharp, confident, well-spaced. Soft, wonky or novelty faces (soft-axis serifs like Fraunces with "soft" on, bubbly or ornamental display) read as casual; keep them for playful, personal or clearly fun briefs.
- **Don't pick (the kit's dislikes):** Didot, Helvetica, Futura, Gotham, Proxima Nova. Exception: an existing client brand that already uses one keeps it. Use the free alternatives instead (e.g. Bodoni Moda or Playfair for Didot's role, Inter or Instrument Sans for Helvetica's, Jost for Futura's, Montserrat or Urbanist for Gotham/Proxima's).
- **Range, not a house font.** Don't reach for the same face as the last build, and don't default to the first name in a group; choose the face whose character fits this brief.
- **Check it loads.** Embedded or self-hosted and rendered when you can; in plain chat, a web-font link plus a fallback stack, stated as unchecked.
- Embed or self-host the free font (see `build-and-deliver.md` §6).
- **Editable Office files are the exception:** decks, Word and Excel files use no-install fonts (Arial, Georgia, Verdana, Trebuchet MS, Arial Black, Impact and others common on Windows and Mac) unless the user asks for or approves custom fonts; work files always do. Pairings: `slides.md` §6. This library is for websites, tools, PDFs and approved custom fonts.

## Groups

### High-end serif (refined, luxury, editorial)
- **Picks (paid):** The Goera, The Choed, Black Saphire, Asfar Serif, Medino, Ginger, Hilden, Melfira, Vanyger, Magnolis, Sevone, Anola, Drose, Garaven, Atfagis, Arunela, Hakuna, Nordish, Cerika, Benso Yolmi (tall condensed serif), Apple Garamond (not licensable at all).
- **Free, embed:** ✦ Playfair Display, ✦ Bodoni Moda, ✦ Cormorant / Cormorant Garamond, ✦ DM Serif Display, ✦ Libre Caslon Display, ✦ Prata, ✦ EB Garamond (text-capable), Gloock, Italiana (thin, fashion), Young Serif (warmer).
- **Use for:** Gallery and Calm & premium pages, fashion, beauty, hospitality, pitches that must feel premium.

### Script (elegant, personal, handwritten)
- **Picks (paid):** Stylish Delight, Violetta, Viana Script, Roselinda Alyamore, Citadel Script (Adobe Fonts), Aston Script, Cayliath (brush), Love, Gullever, Hicheta, Kagesumi (rough brush).
- **Free:** Mr Dafoe (pick, free), Great Vibes, Pinyon Script, Allura, Parisienne, Yellowtail (retro), Caveat Brush / Permanent Marker (brushy).
- **Use for:** one accent word or a signature next to a strong sans or serif. Never for body text or long headlines.

### 70s retro & bubbly (warm, fun, groovy)
- **Picks (paid):** Retro Boldy, Bright, Firlest, Rumble Brave, Ricka, Moste, Trufter, Seatren, Nogams, Starplayer, Atomic Beers, Bringbold Nineties, Sunday Society, Gween Emory, Quirky Quest, Wacky, Maggle, Kateru, Oligeb, Brunch, Fagor, Rugis, Apresto, Beli, Gaon, Kica, Violetta (script side).
- **Free:** MuseoModerno (pick, free), Shrikhand, Chango, Bagel Fat One, Righteous, Gluten, Cherry Bomb One, DynaPuff, Lilita One, Titan One, Sniglet.
- **Use for:** Playful tone, cafés, food, consumer brands, kids, events. Not for professional pitches.

### Tall, loud poster (condensed, punchy)
- **Picks (paid):** Kartyn Condensed, Turnkey Condensed, Montage, DeathStar, Rokiest, Airbus Special (wide italic), Balmonte, Kylira, Sawit, The Bold Font (free version exists; full family paid).
- **Free:** ✦ Anton, ✦ Bebas Neue, ✦ Big Shoulders Display, ✦ Oswald, ✦ League Gothic, ✦ Antonio, Six Caps (ultra-condensed).
- **Use for:** Loud poster, Raw / documentary, sports, music, streetwear, bold headlines.

### Edgy, gothic, grunge
- **Picks (paid):** Toxic Riot (drip), Raventhorn (spiky serif), Dirty Weather, Kagesumi (brush); Pirata One (free).
- **Free:** Pirata One, UnifrakturMaguntia (blackletter), New Rocker, Rubik Wet Paint (drip, novelty), Creepster (novelty; rarely).
- **Use for:** Raw / documentary accents, tattoo *when edgy is asked for*, nightlife, music. One word or wordmark, not whole headings.

### Tech & geometric display
- **Picks (paid):** Gemi, Modern Cosmo, Swoon, Sogea, Bellyvia, Airbus Special, Tactic Sans (wide, bold).
- **Free:** ✦ Unbounded, ✦ Syne, ✦ Michroma, Audiowide, Major Mono Display, Climate Crisis, Monoton (retro lines).
- **Use for:** Technical and Cinematic tones, product and tech launches, gaming.

### Western & ornamental
- **Picks (paid):** Sansdiego, Epstonik, Fodia.
- **Free:** Rye, Sancreek, Ewert.
- **Use for:** a single wordmark or badge (saloons, BBQ, vintage, circus). Special occasions only.

### Everyday sans (body text, UI, clean headlines)
- **Picks:** Nohemi (free download; check commercial terms), Current (paid), Tactic Sans (paid), plus these free ones: ✦ Inter, ✦ Rethink Sans, ✦ Jost, ✦ Urbanist, ✦ Space Grotesk, ✦ Atkinson Hyperlegible (maximum legibility), Montserrat.
- **Also free and good:** ✦ Manrope, ✦ DM Sans, ✦ Instrument Sans, ✦ Hanken Grotesk, Bricolage Grotesque (characterful).
- **Use for:** body copy on almost everything; clean headlines for Practical, Swiss and Technical pages.

## Quick pairings (free)
- Premium pitch: Bodoni Moda or DM Serif Display + Inter.
- Warm local business: Young Serif + Rethink Sans.
- Retro café: Shrikhand + DM Sans.
- Loud poster: Anton + Space Grotesk (+ a mono for labels).
- Edgy / Raw: Big Shoulders Display + Inter, with Pirata One for one word.
- Tech product: Unbounded + Instrument Sans.
- Soft wellness: Cormorant + Jost.

## System stacks (when web fonts can't load or be embedded)
Use these instead of picking a single system face. Style them with care (scale, weight, tight or wide spacing); the font's character matters less than how it's set.

| Role | Stack |
|---|---|
| Serif display (warm, editorial) | `"Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, Georgia, serif` |
| Heavy sans display (poster, confident) | `"Arial Black", "Segoe UI Black", system-ui, sans-serif` with tight letter spacing |
| Condensed display (sporty, loud) | `Bahnschrift, "Avenir Next Condensed", "Arial Narrow", "Roboto Condensed", sans-serif` |
| Text (clean, readable) | `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif` |
| Labels and data (mono) | `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace` |

Never use novelty system faces (Comic Sans, Papyrus, Brush Script, Curlz), and keep Trebuchet and Impact out of body text.
