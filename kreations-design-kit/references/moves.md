# Moves library

Optional moves. Use one when it serves the job and fits the motion level; skip it otherwise. Every move needs a **motion-off version**. "Motion off" and "function off" are different things: with motion off (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, which many Windows users have on), an interactive move must still **work** (choices selectable, drags and clicks responding, totals correct, each state visible). Every move also sits within the motion level chosen from the request.

Pick **one focal point** per page. Several staged scenes are fine only when the ask is a full journey.

## Footer as a final scene (n12, optional)

On brand and story pages the skill **may** turn the footer into a closing scene instead of a plain link list. It's a choice, not a default: skip it on tools, documents, short one-pagers and anything Practical where a neat footer is better. Use at most one variation per page, keep every link readable and clickable, and keep the scene's image in the page's own style (never the reference artwork).

| Variation | Seen | Use when | Reduced motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Illustrated landscape footer**: a big scene fills the footer, with the newsletter field and link columns set directly on it | n12 (Lumina) | Dreamy, travel, wellness, cinematic brands | Static scene |
| **Sticker collage pops in**: a white footer with logo and links, and cut-out illustrations that pop up from the bottom edge as it enters | n12 (Orbit) | Playful and Scrapbook pages, kids, events, space/fun themes | Collage shown at rest |
| **Painted scene with a closing call**: headline + one button at the top of a painted landscape, links along the bottom on a soft fade | n12 (Nexus) | Companies and products that want one last push to act | Static |
| **Link card over a scene**: a white card holding logo, link columns and socials, sitting over a landscape | n12 (Nietzsche) | Calm & premium, sustainability, outdoors | Static |
| **Grid draws in, then the image**: hairline grid lines draw across the footer, then a grainy black-and-white scene fades in above the link table | n12 (Constellation) | Swiss, Technical, developer products | Grid and image shown at rest |

The scene can be code-built (SVG layers, gradients, grain), a marked image slot, or the brand's own art. Contrast behind links must pass; add a scrim if needed.

## Ways to build (suggestions only)

The clips can't show how anything was made. Choose the lightest method that gets the effect:
- **CSS** (transitions, `@keyframes`, scroll-driven animations where supported) for reveals, hovers and simple parallax.
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** for pinned or timed sequences.
- **Canvas / WebGL (Three.js)** for particles, real 3D and shader glows.
- **An image sequence or video** for a rotating product when real 3D isn't worth it.
- **SVG** for line art, diagrams, icons and morphing shapes.

Always add a `prefers-reduced-motion` path, and make sure content is visible at rest (never stuck at `opacity: 0` waiting for a scroll trigger that might not fire).
