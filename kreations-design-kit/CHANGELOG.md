## 1.0.0 (2026-09-27)
First public release of the Kreations Design Kit.

- **Covers:** websites, landing pages and portfolios; HTML tools and calculators; designed documents; slide decks.
- **Built from:** 22 website concepts, 11 live sites, 12 reference decks, a curated font library, about 60 mood palettes, and 84 contrast-checked palette sets across 15 kinds of project.
- **Tested:** over many rounds of real test builds, with independent AI reviews at each checkpoint, on Claude and ChatGPT Work.
- **Also included:**
  - a condensed lite version for small local models (`lite/`);
  - a transitions helper for PowerPoint files (`scripts/pptx_motion.py`);
  - setup notes for Open WebUI (`agents/open-webui-setup.md`);
  - an optional personal add-on: copy `personal.example.md` to `personal.md` to make the kit yours.
